Privacy Policy for Pitchpole

Last updated 12 August 2026 · Published by Portal Crafter

Pitchpole is a game with no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics. It does not ask for your name, your email address or your location, and it has nowhere to send them if it did. Your progress lives on your device.

Two things do reach the outside world, and this policy is mostly about them: the advertising in the game, and the optional sign in to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center. This policy covers the Pitchpole mobile app on Android and iOS.

What the game itself stores

All of this is written to your device's own app storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere, none of it is backed up to us, and we cannot read it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

  • How many stars you earned on each level, and your best time on it.
  • How many coins you collected on each level.
  • Your settings: sound and music on or off and how loud, vibration on or off, and which of the two control schemes you picked.
  • If you sign in to Play Games or Game Center, the display name that service gave us, so the button can show who is signed in without asking the service again every time the game opens.

You can erase all of it from inside the game: Settings → Reset progress clears every star and time, and Forget this account removes the stored display name and stops the game reconnecting.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by advertising, served by Google AdMob. It shows a banner on the level select screen, an occasional full screen ad between levels, and an ad you can choose to watch in exchange for an extra life. You are never required to watch one to continue playing.

To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device. Google, not Portal Crafter, decides what is collected and processes it. Typically that includes:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, a resettable ID your phone provides for this purpose.
  • Device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version and the app you are using.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate region can be derived.
  • Whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped.

We do not receive any of this and we cannot identify you from it. What Google does with it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings: on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, and on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Signing in to Play Games or Game Center

This is entirely optional, and the whole game plays identically without it. The game never signs you in on its own: nothing is sent to either service until you press the button yourself.

If you do sign in, Google Play Games (on Android) or Apple Game Center (on iOS) tells the game your display name and profile picture. The game shows them on the button and stores the name on your device. It does not upload your scores, times or coins, because the game has no servers to upload them to. Your use of those services is governed by Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting.
  • We do not access your photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no payment details are ever handled.

Children

Pitchpole has no chat, no user accounts, no user generated content and no way to spend money. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Where the app is distributed to an audience that includes children, ads are configured to be limited to content rated suitable for all ages and to be served without personalisation, in line with Google Play's Families policy and applicable children's privacy law including COPPA and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

Your rights

Because the game holds no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on request. Everything the game stores is on your device and is under your control: reset it in settings, or uninstall the app to remove it entirely. For the advertising identifier, use your device's own controls, described above.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the legal basis for serving personalised ads is your consent, which is requested by Google's consent flow where it applies, and which you can change or withdraw at any time through that flow. Non-personalised ads are served on the basis of legitimate interest in funding a free game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the store listing.
