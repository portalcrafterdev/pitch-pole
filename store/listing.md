# Play Store listing copy

Every field the Play Console asks for on the main store listing page, with the
character limits it enforces. Paste straight in.

Nothing here claims a feature the app does not have. That is not only honesty:
Play removes apps for listings that misrepresent them, and the usual way it
happens is a description written from what was planned rather than from what
shipped. In particular this copy does **not** mention leaderboards or
achievements, because the game signs in to Play Games for identity only and
posts nothing to it.

## Full description

Limit 4000 characters. This is about 1900.

```
Your runner never stops. One tap flips gravity so it falls up to the ceiling
and runs upside down, another jumps off whichever surface it is standing on.
That is the whole game, and there are 1500 levels of it.

RUN, FLIP, JUMP
The forest comes at you at speed. Read what is ahead, pick a surface, and
commit. Every obstacle is answered a different way:

- Bolted enemies are stuck to one surface. Flip to the other one.
- Hoppers bounce on a rhythm. Run under them, or jump them while they rest.
- Blades sweep the whole gap. Be on the side they are furthest from.
- Stones slam down and are winched back. Wait, or be past before they drop.
- Fire vents glow before they light. Be on the other surface while they burn.
- Bats hover in the middle. A jump peaks inside one and a flip crosses it, so
  this is the one you decide before you get there.
- Spiders drop from the ceiling on a thread. Stay on the floor, and do not
  jump.

1500 LEVELS THAT ALL FINISH
Every level was checked by a solver before it shipped, so not one of them is
impossible. Coins are laid along a route that provably works: follow a row of
them and you are on a line that reaches the door.

BUILT TO BE FAIR
The game runs on a fixed timestep, so a level behaves exactly the same on a
60Hz phone and a 120Hz one. Hit boxes are drawn smaller than the sprites, so
nothing ever kills you from further away than it looks. Checkpoints put every
moving obstacle back exactly where it was.

PICK YOUR CONTROLS
Screen halves, where the left flips and the right jumps and there is nothing
small to miss. Or three pads, if you would rather have buttons. Sound, music
and vibration each have their own switch.

COLLECT AND COMPARE
Three stars and a best time on every level. Coins to sweep up on the way past.
Nothing to buy with them: there is no shop and no currency, they are there to
collect.

NO PURCHASES
There are no in-app purchases and nothing is locked behind a payment. The game
is supported by ads. Sign in with Google Play Games if you want to, or do not:
every level plays identically either way, and your stars and times are saved on
your device regardless.

Landscape, both ways round. Plays offline.
```

The icon and the feature graphic are generated by `tool/make_store_art.dart`,
which draws the feature graphic with the game's own scene code. Re-run it after
any change to the menu art and the banner follows:

```bash
flutter test tool/make_store_art.dart
```

The screenshots are captured off a running build rather than generated, because
a screenshot is meant to be evidence. Play wants at least 2 and takes up to 8.

There is no tablet screenshot set yet. Without one, Play will still list the
game but flags it as not optimised for large screens.
